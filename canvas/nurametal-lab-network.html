Prerequisites for both paths: (1) Prism vSwitch Forged Transmits enabled on both VLAN101 and VLAN201 vSwitches. (2) 10G Switch VLAN101 SVI: ip helper-address 172.16.0.65  |  400G Switch VLAN201 SVI: ip helper-address 172.16.10.65. (3) Ubuntu VM eth1 in VLAN101 · eth2 in VLAN201 with static IPs. (4) NICo deployed with values.physical.yaml (DHCP/PXE/DNS enabled, mocks disabled).
